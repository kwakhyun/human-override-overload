# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

Build app UI in `src/`. Keep `.openai/hosting.json`, `worker/index.js`, `scripts/prepare-sites-build.mjs`, and `tests/sites-worker.test.mjs` intact so the same local prototype can be handed to Sites. Before a Sites handoff, run `npm run build` and `npm run test:sites`; the build must leave `dist/client/index.html`, `dist/server/index.js`, and `dist/.openai/hosting.json`.

## Durable project decisions

- The active browser runtime is Phaser 4.2.1 with TypeScript and Vite. `src/phaser/` owns boot/loading, the battle scene, WebGL rendering, forward-scrolling route presentation, camera orchestration, pointer/keyboard/touch input, sprite pooling, telegraphs, and combat VFX. React remains responsible for the intro, minimal in-canvas DOM HUD, cinematic dialogue panel, reward modal, accessibility labels, and result screen. Keep the deterministic rules in `src/swarm/engine.js` behind `SceneBridge`; do not move combat authority into Phaser sprite or tween state.
- Treat `src/game/assets/manifest.ts` as the only active runtime asset-path registry. The previous custom Canvas renderer in `src/swarm/renderer.js` may remain for provenance and tests, but the active App must not call `renderSwarm` or mount a hand-authored combat canvas unless the user explicitly asks to roll back the Phaser migration.
- The user's latest explicit replacement direction always supersedes earlier core-loop proposals. The active game is `TRAIN ME WRONG: OVERLOAD`, a single-character forward-expedition survivor campaign. The first WRONG ENGINE expedition uses a fixed 300-hostile route before its independent boss room; unlocked follow-up regions may retain their fixed 1,000-hostile routes. The first route also contains ROOK/NYX/MOSS traces. Historical arena and four-region prototype code may remain for provenance, but it must not influence the active UI, controls, pacing, economy, or documentation unless the user explicitly asks to restore it.
- Keep one controllable player actor on screen. Movement is WASD or arrow keys, Space dashes, and pointer movement aims. The basic weapon always uses continuous pointer-directed automatic fire. There is no automatic-fire toggle, `E` fire-toggle binding, pointer-click manual volley, or equivalent touch toggle; `E` is reserved for AEGIS WARD. Do not restore the discarded fire modes unless the user explicitly reverses this decision.
- Space is a true phase dash with at least 0.46 seconds of damage immunity so expanding boss rings can be crossed intentionally. Manual abilities are independent base-kit edge-triggered actions: pointer-targeted `Q` EMP PULSE stops normal mechanical enemies for 3.6 seconds and elites for 1.8 seconds without pulling enemies or bending projectiles, `E` AEGIS WARD grants a moving five-second heal/shield/status-defense field, `F` STRATOS RUN sends three sequential parallel strafing lanes through the pointer, and `R` HELIX TEMPEST spends a 72-second cooldown on four engine-owned lances rotating rapidly through 360 degrees for 3.2 seconds. They expose separate cooldown/ready state and share one controller path with mobile touch buttons. They must not reuse the level-up airstrike/Omega entities, events, timers, ranks, or VFX. Arc, Nova, automatic Skyfall support, and automatic Omega Laser remain separate level-up build systems. The discarded `4-FRONT RECALL`, `queueRecall`, temporary four-companion summon, and NULL SNARE gravity behavior must not be restored unless the user explicitly reverses this decision. Persistent ally build choices remain separate autonomous combat options.
- Use a fixed total of exactly 300 enemies for the first WRONG ENGINE expedition; follow-up regions may retain exactly 1,000. Keep every opening readable and raise the active pressure cap with AEGIS level, clear progress, and elapsed combat time rather than dumping the full budget at spawn. Every route enemy must materialize through one of five visible SOVEREIGN transit gates with a gate-opening animation and non-damaging spawn delay, never directly in empty air around AEGIS. Reaching the first bulkhead while hostiles remain emits a Korean kill-all prompt and keeps the entrance locked. Clearing the selected route budget starts the finite automatic clear sequence: 1.2 seconds of simultaneous warning graphics/effects, then 1.6 seconds of AEGIS panic presentation, followed by the independent boss-room transition. Do not restore a manual entry-decision modal or a post-clear right-arrow walk requirement.
- Keep the three SOVEREIGN field roles mechanically distinct: hunter drones chase and self-destruct for a single large critical contact hit, suppressor infantry are medium-range riflemen with visible three-to-five-round bursts, and brute platforms are long-range snipers that lock one engine-owned warning lane before firing a high-speed rail shot down exactly that line. Distribute fourteen deterministic-but-randomized healing kits across the route; contact restores hull once and removes the kit. These roles, item positions, damage, and collision timing remain simulation-owned rather than Phaser-owned.
- Preserve the region-specific deterministic compositions in `REGION_ENEMY_PROFILES`: WRONG ENGINE opens at 60/30/10 hunter/suppressor/brute and reinforces at 50/30/20; GLASS DUNE is sniper-heavy at 20/30/50 then 10/30/60; ABYSSAL ARCHIVE is suicide-drone-heavy at 80/10/10 then 70/20/10. Do not flatten these back into one global modulo sequence; use the region's own 300/1,000 total budget while preserving growth-capped five-gate spawning.
- Present combat through a player-centered zoomed camera and a horizontally scrolling route. AEGIS remains at the exact screen center during route and boss combat; stage zoom and the user's mouse-wheel zoom-in factor may change without dislodging that center. Keep one compact tactical minimap at the lower-right on desktop and below the top HUD on mobile landscape: it plots the player, a bounded sample of live enemies, active transit gates, and the boss/route endpoint with minimal text. Do not restore the old outside-canvas telemetry rail; route objective, health, XP, boss/enemy status, dash, Q/E/F/R active cooldowns, sound, and this tactical map are the only persistent HUD information.
- The active route simulation is a 13,200×1,080 world with 12,000 units of actual forward distance; the independent boss chamber remains 1,920×1,080. Route progress derives from AEGIS's real world x coordinate, not a second right-input texture-scroll counter, so left/right speed stays symmetric and long-range shots are not clipped by the old room bounds. Constrain AEGIS to the authored center corridor: the lower cliff/void and other visually blocked margins are non-traversable. Normal combat uses a 1.08 follow zoom before user wheel adjustment, and the route hard-stops at the early entrance while any selected-budget hostile or live enemy entity remains. Keep the locked gate in sector 3; sector 4 and the boss-room texture must never leak or reveal an unpainted black edge before the clear sequence begins.
- Treat every route and its boss chamber as two independent stages. Do not load boss-room art, teleport actors, start encounter dialogue, or activate the boss while route hostiles remain. When the selected regional budget reaches zero, freeze hostile combat, show the 1.2-second multi-layer warning and 1.6-second AEGIS panic beat, then automatically fade the route out and load the independent chamber—there is no entry approval/decline modal. Place AEGIS at x=820 and the selected regional boss at x=1390, then fade the boss stage in. This placement keeps AEGIS centered without exposing an unpainted camera edge and brings most of the giant boss silhouette into the expanded view.
- Escalate each region's finite enemy budget with explicitly warned growth-capped mass-surges appropriate to its 300- or 1,000-unit route. The warning must precede the gate burst, remain readable over combat, and be reinforced by red edge treatment and sound. At 48/72/88% clear progress, unlock increasingly strong overdrive tiers that accelerate attacks and culminate in automatic master-scale pulse volleys.
- Enemy kills grant XP. The first reward must occur within 6–10 seconds, but later XP thresholds may be banked into one stronger choice. Enforce a meaningful span of active combat between reward overlays; never open consecutive modals as soon as the previous choice closes. Each offer must contain weapon, skill, and ally options of comparable immediate combat value, and a batched choice may grant multiple ranks at once.
- Include repeatable offensive skill progression and visible mastery transformations. Arc Cascade, Zero-Point Nova, Skyfall air support, and the Omega Laser must all have real combat effects; long-cooldown ultimates require telegraph, impact, cooldown HUD, and materially higher crowd-clearing power.
