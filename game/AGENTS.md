# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

Build app UI in `src/`. Keep `.openai/hosting.json`, `worker/index.js`, `scripts/prepare-sites-build.mjs`, and `tests/sites-worker.test.mjs` intact so the same local prototype can be handed to Sites. Before a Sites handoff, run `npm run build` and `npm run test:sites`; the build must leave `dist/client/index.html`, `dist/server/index.js`, and `dist/.openai/hosting.json`.

## Durable project decisions

- The active browser runtime is Phaser 4.2.1 with TypeScript and Vite. `src/phaser/` owns boot/loading, the battle scene, WebGL rendering, forward-scrolling route presentation, camera orchestration, pointer/keyboard/touch input, sprite pooling, telegraphs, and combat VFX. React remains responsible for the intro, minimal in-canvas DOM HUD, cinematic dialogue panel, reward modal, accessibility labels, and result screen. Keep the deterministic rules in `src/swarm/engine.js` behind `SceneBridge`; do not move combat authority into Phaser sprite or tween state.
- Treat `src/game/assets/manifest.ts` as the only active runtime asset-path registry. The previous custom Canvas renderer in `src/swarm/renderer.js` may remain for provenance and tests, but the active App must not call `renderSwarm` or mount a hand-authored combat canvas unless the user explicitly asks to roll back the Phaser migration.
- The user's latest explicit replacement direction always supersedes earlier core-loop proposals. The active game is `TRAIN ME WRONG: OVERLOAD`, a single-character forward-expedition survivor campaign. Each sortie preserves the 1,000-hostile route-to-independent-boss-room loop; the first route also contains ROOK/NYX/MOSS traces. Historical arena and four-region prototype code may remain for provenance, but it must not influence the active UI, controls, pacing, economy, or documentation unless the user explicitly asks to restore it.
- Keep one controllable player actor on screen. Movement is WASD or arrow keys, Space dashes, and pointer movement aims. The basic weapon always uses continuous pointer-directed automatic fire. There is no automatic-fire toggle, `E` fire-toggle binding, pointer-click manual volley, or equivalent touch toggle; `E` is reserved for AEGIS WARD. Do not restore the discarded fire modes unless the user explicitly reverses this decision.
- Space is a true phase dash with at least 0.46 seconds of damage immunity so expanding boss rings can be crossed intentionally. Manual abilities are independent base-kit edge-triggered actions: `Q` NULL SNARE deploys a pointer gravity field for pull/slow and projectile bending, `E` AEGIS WARD grants a moving five-second heal/shield/status-defense field, `F` STRATOS RUN sends three sequential parallel strafing lanes through the pointer, and `R` HELIX TEMPEST spends a 72-second cooldown on four engine-owned lances rotating rapidly through 360 degrees for 3.2 seconds. They expose separate cooldown/ready state and share one controller path with mobile touch buttons. They must not reuse the level-up airstrike/Omega entities, events, timers, ranks, or VFX. Arc, Nova, automatic Skyfall support, and automatic Omega Laser remain separate level-up build systems. The discarded `4-FRONT RECALL`, `queueRecall`, and temporary four-companion summon path must not be restored unless the user explicitly reverses this decision. Persistent ally build choices remain separate autonomous combat options.
- Use a fixed total of exactly 1,000 enemies, but keep the opening readable: 36 hostiles deploy first and the active pressure cap rises from 36 toward 220 according to AEGIS level, clear progress, and elapsed combat time. Queue warned 124/180/260/400-unit reinforcement tiers at 9.25/34.5/67.5/105.5 seconds; queued enemies may fill only the current growth-scaled pressure cap. Every route enemy must materialize through one of five visible SOVEREIGN transit gates with a gate-opening animation and non-damaging spawn delay, never directly in empty air around AEGIS. Zero remaining enemies unlocks the engine bulkhead, while the player-confirmed stage transition preserves a finite clear-to-boss loop.
- Keep the three SOVEREIGN field roles mechanically distinct: hunter drones chase and self-destruct for a single large critical contact hit, suppressor infantry are medium-range riflemen with visible three-to-five-round bursts, and brute platforms are long-range snipers that lock one engine-owned warning lane before firing a high-speed rail shot down exactly that line. Distribute fourteen deterministic-but-randomized healing kits across the route; contact restores hull once and removes the kit. These roles, item positions, damage, and collision timing remain simulation-owned rather than Phaser-owned.
- Preserve the region-specific deterministic compositions in `REGION_ENEMY_PROFILES`: WRONG ENGINE opens at 60/30/10 hunter/suppressor/brute and reinforces at 50/30/20; GLASS DUNE is sniper-heavy at 20/30/50 then 10/30/60; ABYSSAL ARCHIVE is suicide-drone-heavy at 80/10/10 then 70/20/10. Do not flatten these back into one global modulo sequence; total budget, growth cap, and five-gate spawning remain unchanged.
- Present combat through a player-centered zoomed camera and a horizontally scrolling route. AEGIS remains at the exact screen center during both the 1,000-enemy route and boss encounter; only camera zoom changes by stage. Keep one compact in-frame route minimap at the lower-right on desktop and below the top HUD on mobile landscape: it shows AEGIS progress, available squad-trace checkpoints, the boss node, and an eastbound next-target instruction. Do not restore the old outside-canvas telemetry rail; route objective, health, XP, boss/enemy status, dash, Q/E/F/R active cooldowns, sound, and this route navigator are the only persistent HUD information.
- The active route simulation is a 13,200×1,080 world with 12,000 units of actual traversable forward distance; the independent boss chamber remains 1,920×1,080. Route progress derives from AEGIS's real world x coordinate, not a second right-input texture-scroll counter, so left/right speed stays symmetric and long-range shots are not clipped by the old room bounds. Normal combat uses a 1.08 follow zoom, and the route hard-stops at 10,680 until all 1,000 spawned hostiles are dead and no live enemy entity remains. Keep the locked gate in sector 3; sector 4 and the boss-room texture must never appear before that condition is satisfied.
- Treat each 1,000-hostile route and its boss chamber as two independent stages. Reaching the unlocked bulkhead freezes the run and emits one explicit entry-decision modal; do not load the boss-room art, teleport actors, start the encounter dialogue, or activate the boss until the player confirms. Declining the modal keeps time frozen and exposes a small reopen action. On confirmation, fade the route out, place AEGIS at x=820 and the selected regional boss at x=1390 in the separate chamber, then fade the boss stage in. This placement keeps AEGIS centered without exposing an unpainted camera edge and brings most of the giant boss silhouette into the expanded view.
- Escalate the 1,000-enemy phase with explicitly warned growth-capped mass-surges at the fixed run times above. The warning must precede the gate burst, remain readable over combat, and be reinforced by red edge treatment and sound. At 48/72/88% clear progress, unlock increasingly strong overdrive tiers that accelerate attacks and culminate in automatic master-scale pulse volleys.
- Enemy kills grant XP. The first reward must occur within 6–10 seconds, but later XP thresholds may be banked into one stronger choice. Enforce a meaningful span of active combat between reward overlays; never open consecutive modals as soon as the previous choice closes. Each offer must contain weapon, skill, and ally options of comparable immediate combat value, and a batched choice may grant multiple ranks at once.
- Include repeatable offensive skill progression and visible mastery transformations. Arc Cascade, Zero-Point Nova, Skyfall air support, and the Omega Laser must all have real combat effects; long-cooldown ultimates require telegraph, impact, cooldown HUD, and materially higher crowd-clearing power. Master ranks should visibly flood the nearby battlefield with upgraded area attacks.
- After each regional swarm is cleared, transition to its giant boss. Its HP budget must sustain a real multi-pattern encounter even against a late-game mastered build. It must visibly transform into dedicated phase-2 armor-break and phase-3 core-meltdown sprites at 70% and 38% HP. Each transformation briefly locks damage, emits repeated high-priority warnings, and escalates projectile density, dual sweeps, ring count, warning speed, rapid charges, and autonomous rage bursts. Each encounter must combine distinctly telegraphed radial fire, sweeps, delayed blast zones, expanding rings, single charges, and a rapid multi-charge that briefly relocks before every rush. Dodging the final charge into the arena boundary exposes the core for three seconds and applies a real 2× damage multiplier. Direct contact with the boss body or a charge is a critical hit, briefly stuns movement, dash, and basic fire, and visibly separates the player so the collision cannot silently multi-hit.
- Keep each follow-up boss's two signature patterns in addition to the six common patterns: MIRROR TYRANT owns `prismLattice` and `solarFlare`; DROWNED ORACLE owns `memorySpiral` and `depthCollapse`. The engine's lanes, ordered targets, rotating segments, and contracting radii are the single collision/telegraph geometry; Phaser must render those same mutable objects rather than approximate them with unrelated tweens.
- Preserve readable combat feedback at crowd scale: remaining-enemy count, route progress, XP progress, level-up choices, boss entrance, danger telegraphs, weak-point exposure, stun/contact feedback, hit-stop, damage feedback, and victory state must remain legible. Keep the center and lower-middle playfield clear during normal play; only scenario dialogue may temporarily occupy the bottom of the screen while simulation is paused.
