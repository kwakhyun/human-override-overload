# Suno AI BGM prompts

아래 프롬프트는 모두 `Instrumental` 모드용입니다. 한 곡당 2분 30초~3분 30초 길이로 생성한 뒤,
게임에 사용할 최종 파일은 시작·끝이 자연스럽게 이어지는 루프 버전을 선택합니다. 특정 아티스트나
기존 게임 음악을 모방하지 않고 `HUMAN OVERRIDE: OVERLOAD`의 지역 정체성만 설명합니다.

## 헤이븐-09 메인 기지 로비

추천 제목: `Last Light in Haven-09`

```text
Instrumental cinematic sci-fi ambient music for the main lobby of HAVEN-09, humanity's last underground command base in a world ruled by a sovereign AI. Calm but never completely safe. 78 BPM, D minor, 4/4, seamless game loop, restrained dynamics. Warm felt piano fragments and distant solo cello represent the remaining human survivors; cold analog synth pads, low machine pulses, filtered radio noise, soft relay clicks and subtle modular arpeggios represent the AI war outside. Begin with a quiet four-bar atmosphere, introduce a memorable three-note human motif, then slowly add a controlled tactical pulse without becoming combat music. Spacious, intimate, melancholic, cautiously hopeful, high-tech military shelter at night. Clean mix with room for Korean dialogue and UI sounds, no vocals, no lyrics, no choir, no huge trailer drums, no heroic brass, no EDM drop, no jump scares, no abrupt ending.
```

## 2구역 유리 사구 · GLASS DUNE

추천 제목: `Refraction War`

```text
Instrumental high-intensity sci-fi combat music for GLASS DUNE, a blinding desert of black glass, buried solar observatories and mirror-machine snipers controlled by a sovereign AI. 132 BPM, D Phrygian, aggressive but readable seamless game loop. Combine tight industrial breakbeats, dry distorted bass pulses, granular glass percussion, razor-thin synthetic plucked motifs, bowed metal resonance, heat-haze synth layers and precise syncopated warning rhythms. The music should feel like sunlight refracting into lethal targeting beams: sharp, dry, tactical, elegant and hostile. Build from an eight-bar stalking pulse into sustained combat momentum, include a short low-density bridge for dialogue readability, then return cleanly to the main loop. Modern cybernetic production, wide stereo glass details but centered low end, no vocals, no lyrics, no ethnic pastiche, no festival EDM drop, no cheerful melody, no oversized trailer brass, no fade-out.
```

## 3구역 심해 기록고 · ABYSSAL ARCHIVE

추천 제목: `Memory Below Pressure`

```text
Instrumental dark sci-fi combat music for ABYSSAL ARCHIVE, a drowned machine memory vault far below the ruined city where corrupted AI records move like deep-sea organisms. 108 BPM with a heavy half-time feel, C-sharp minor with restrained dissonance, seamless game loop. Use immense sub-bass pressure, distant sonar pings, submerged metallic impacts, granular synthetic textures, slow rotating polyrhythms, cold pulse sequences and a wordless synthetic spectral pad that never sounds like a human singer. The groove should feel patient, crushing and intelligent rather than chaotic; gradually reveal faster machine percussion and distorted data pulses as danger rises. Include a brief hollow-pressure break, then return to the main combat pattern without a hard cut. Claustrophobic, ancient, biomechanical, mysterious and threatening, clear transient space for weapon SFX and English system voice lines. No vocals, no lyrics, no acoustic drums, no pirate or nautical adventure mood, no horror jump scares, no bright major chords, no EDM drop, no fade-out.
```

## 적용 슬롯

- 타이틀: 사용자 제공 `잿빛 하늘 아래` 적용 완료
- 헤이븐-09 로비: 사용자 제공 `Last Light in Haven-09.mp3` 적용 완료
- 1구역 오답 엔진 중앙로: 기존 `overload-main-theme.mp3`만 사용
- 2구역 유리 사구: 사용자 제공 `2구역_Refraction War.mp3` 적용 완료
- 3구역 심해 기록고: 사용자 제공 `3구역_Memory Below Pressure.mp3` 적용 완료
- 4~6구역 외곽 생산권역: 전용 음원이 아직 없어 현재 무음 전투로 처리

이 문서는 현재 적용된 5개 트랙의 제작 브리프입니다. 외곽 권역용 음악을 추가할 때는 기존
1~3구역 파일을 복제하지 말고, 각 지역의 고유 전투 정체성과 새 런타임 경로를 함께 기록합니다.
